# The Painfully Honest Out-of-Office

An entry for the [make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/u-po-pu-the-painfully-honest-out-of-office-63e7ca0b): one out-of-office email auto-reply, at most 200 characters, painfully honest about the sender's absence and inbox — and still polite enough that a manager would let it stand.

`main.py` deterministically writes the reply to `/output/entry.txt`.

> Thank you for your email. I'm out of the office until Monday, and so is my motivation — your message is 47th in line behind my will to live.
